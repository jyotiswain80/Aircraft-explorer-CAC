Airliner history

Key milestones that shaped the modern jet airliner.

  1. 1903 Wright Flyer: first sustained, powered, controlled heavier-than-air flight. This aircraft was the product of a four year research by Wilbur and Orville Wright. It failed once after it was built, but three days later they were able to fly the plane. this marked the beginning of a new chapter.
  2. 1914 Benoist XIV: first scheduled commercial passenger airline flight. Over 3,000 people came to the waterfront to watch the flight takeoff.
  3. 1935 Douglas DC-3: all-metal, reliable, made passenger air travel profitable. This aircraft was able to carry up to 6,000 pounds of cargo, and could carry 21 to 35 passengers. it was able to go distaces like 1,500 to 2,100 miles.
  4. 1952 de Havilland Comet: first commercial jet airliner in passenger service.
  5. 1958 Boeing 707: swept wings, podded engines, ushered in the Jet Age.
  6. 1967 Boeing 737: original twinjet design, now the best-selling airliner in history.
  7. 1969 Boeing 747: first wide-body jumbo jet, made international travel accessible.
  8. 1988 Airbus A320: introduced digital fly-by-wire flight controls to airliners.
  9. 2017 737 MAX: high-bypass LEAP-1B turbofans, advanced winglets, modern flight deck.
